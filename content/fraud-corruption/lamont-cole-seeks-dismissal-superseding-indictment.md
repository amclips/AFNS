**Status: Alleged.** These are allegations. Cole has not been convicted and has moved to have the charges dismissed.

## What happened

A grand jury issued a superseding indictment that narrows the bribery case against LaMont Cole, a former Baton Rouge mayor pro tem who now leads the city's schools.

## What changed

The original case centered on about $40K in cash and gifts allegedly connected to roughly $600K in contracts. The superseding indictment drops those references.

Cole now faces one count each of:

- bribery conspiracy
- money laundering
- malfeasance

## What's next

Cole is asking the court to throw out the charges.
