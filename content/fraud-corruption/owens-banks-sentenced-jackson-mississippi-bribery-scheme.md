**Status: Sentenced Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026.** Both defendants pleaded guilty. Coverage dated to the sentencing day (Mississippi Today, Clarion Ledger); some wire reprints carried early Nov. 6 UTC stamps.

Former Hinds County District Attorney **Jody Owens** was sentenced to **41 months** in federal prison and a **$100,000** fine, and former Jackson Councilman **Aaron Banks** to **45 months** and a **$15,000** fine, for their roles in a Jackson bribery scheme ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/hinds-county-da-mississippi-bribery-scheme-9cf86e22b808813991c4ac28ddaec9b1), [Mississippi Today](https://mississippitoday.org/2026/11/05/jackson-federal-corruption-sentencing/), [Clarion Ledger](https://www.clarionledger.com/story/news/2026/11/05/jody-owens-former-hinds-county-da-gets-41-months-in-bribery-case/92628380007/), [Clarion Ledger](https://www.clarionledger.com/story/news/local/2026/11/05/former-jackson-ms-councilman-aaron-banks-gets-prison-sentence-in-bribe-case/92629524007/)). U.S. District Judge Daniel P. Jordan III called Owens the **"ringleader."**

The case grew from a **2023 undercover FBI sting** in which agents posed as out-of-state developers seeking a downtown convention-center hotel. Owens was accused of soliciting about **$250,000** in bribes to rig city contract bidding. Banks, who pleaded guilty to conspiracy, took **$10,000** cash plus benefits that included a job for his daughter, the Clarion Ledger reports. Prosecutors had sought 51-60 months for Banks; his longer sentence relative to Owens partly reflected prior DUI convictions ([WLBT](https://www.wlbt.com/2026/11/05/i-knew-better-aaron-banks-sentenced-jackson-bribery-case/)).

Former Mayor **Chokwe Antar Lumumba** pleaded guilty last summer to one conspiracy count and awaits sentencing. Owens is to report to the Yazoo City federal complex on Jan. 4, 2027; Banks remains out on bond until January, Mississippi Today reports.
