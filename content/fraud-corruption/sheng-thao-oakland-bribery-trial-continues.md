**Status: On trial.** These are allegations. All four defendants have pleaded not guilty, and the trial continues.

## What happened

On Monday, defense attorneys in former Oakland Mayor Sheng Thao's federal bribery trial cross-examined an FBI agent and pressed on whether recordings central to the government's case are authentic.

## The allegations

Prosecutors allege that members of the Duong family, who own Oakland's recycling contractor, supported Thao's mayoral campaign in return for official actions benefiting them.
