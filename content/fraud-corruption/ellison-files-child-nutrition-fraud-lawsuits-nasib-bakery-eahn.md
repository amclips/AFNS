**Status: Alleged (civil lawsuits).** These are civil claims, not criminal charges, and have not been proven in court.

## What happened

Minnesota Attorney General Ellison filed two more lawsuits over alleged child-nutrition fraud:

- **Nasib Bakery** allegedly claimed more than $2.8 million for meals it never served.
- **East African Healthcare Network** allegedly claimed more than $10 million for over 3 million meals in 2020-22.

## What's next

The lawsuits seek triple damages.
