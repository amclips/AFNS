**Status: Alleged (civil lawsuits).** These are civil allegations, not criminal charges or convictions. Neither defendant has been found liable, and the claims have not been proven in court.

## What happened

On Oct. 26, Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison filed two more civil lawsuits over alleged child-nutrition fraud, totaling about $12.8 million in alleged claims (Minnesota Attorney General's Office; CBS Minnesota):

- **Nasib Bakery** allegedly claimed more than $2.8 million for meals it never served.
- **East African Healthcare Network** allegedly claimed more than $10 million for over 3 million meals in 2020-22.

## What's next

The lawsuits seek triple damages. Neither defendant has been found liable.
