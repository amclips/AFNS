**Status: Convicted.**

## What happened

A jury returned six guilty verdicts against Abdifatah Yusuf of Promise Health Services in a $7.2 million Medicaid fraud case. A Hennepin County judge then threw out those verdicts.

A unanimous Minnesota appeals court panel reversed her and reinstated the verdicts. According to the opinion, Yusuf personally took in more than $1 million.

## What's next

The appeals court sent the case back for sentencing.
