**Status: Convicted.** The guilty verdicts are reinstated, and the case goes back for sentencing.

## What happened

A jury convicted Abdifatah Yusuf of Promise Health Services on six counts tied to $7.2 million in Medicaid fraud, but a Hennepin County judge set the verdicts aside. Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison's office appealed.

On October 26 a unanimous Court of Appeals panel reversed the trial judge, reinstated the guilty verdicts, and sent the case back for sentencing. According to the opinion, Yusuf personally received more than $1 million.
