**Status: Alleged.** These are allegations. Neither has been convicted. Their lawyers call the charges political.

## What happened

The U.S. Attorney's Office in Washington, D.C. charged Nikki Floris and Bradley Benavides with making false statements to Congress. Each faces one count.

Prosecutors say that when the House Judiciary Committee questioned them in 2023, both denied any involvement in the Hunter Biden investigations, and that those denials were false.

## Who they are

Both held senior posts on the FBI's Foreign Influence Task Force, which handled foreign influence threats.

## The defense

Defense lawyers say the case is politically motivated.
