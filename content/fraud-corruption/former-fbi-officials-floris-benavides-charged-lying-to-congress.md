**Status: Alleged.** Both have been charged, not convicted. Their lawyers call the charges political.

## What happened

Two former senior FBI officials, Nikki Floris and Bradley Benavides, were charged with lying to Congress. Both led the FBI's Foreign Influence Task Force.

Prosecutors say they falsely told the House Judiciary Committee in 2023 that they had no role in the Hunter Biden investigations.
