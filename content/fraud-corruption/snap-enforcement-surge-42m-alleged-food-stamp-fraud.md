**Status: Mostly alleged.** Most of the cases are allegations that have not been proven in court.

## What happened

On October 21, the Justice Department, the U.S. Department of Agriculture and USDA's Office of Inspector General announced an enforcement surge targeting about $42 million in alleged fraud in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), commonly known as food stamps.

The actions were brought against more than 40 defendants in 13 U.S. Attorney districts.
