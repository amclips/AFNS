**Status: Mostly alleged.** Most of the defendants are charged but not convicted.

## What happened

On October 21, DOJ's Fraud Section, USDA, and USDA's inspector general announced a coordinated round of enforcement against fraud in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), the federal food-stamp program.

The actions name more than 40 defendants in 13 U.S. Attorney districts, with alleged losses of nearly $42 million.

## The White House angle

The Washington Examiner reported that the vice president's anti-fraud task force highlighted the findings.
