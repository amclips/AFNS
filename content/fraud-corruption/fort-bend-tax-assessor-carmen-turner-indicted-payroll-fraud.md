**Status: Alleged.** Turner has been indicted but not convicted.

## The allegations

The U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Texas says Turner used her office's payroll to fund private care for her 81-year-old mother by hiring two people as deputy tax clerks who did not do county work.

Prosecutors also say she put a man on the payroll who was working full-time in Chicago and required him to return part of his county wages to her.

## The charges

The indictment charges conspiracy, wire fraud, and theft from a program receiving federal funds. Turner remains in office while the case moves forward.
