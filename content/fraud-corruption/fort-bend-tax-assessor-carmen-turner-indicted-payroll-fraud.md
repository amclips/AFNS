**Status: Alleged.** Turner has been indicted but not convicted.

## What happened

Carmen Turner (D), the tax assessor for Fort Bend County, Texas, was indicted over alleged payroll fraud.

She allegedly put two "deputy tax clerks" on the county payroll to care for her 81-year-old mother. She also allegedly hired a man who already worked full-time in Chicago and had him kick back part of his pay.

## The charges

Turner faces conspiracy, wire fraud and federal-program theft charges. She is still in office.
