**Status: Charged October 22.** The charges are allegations; the defendants have not been convicted.

## What happened

The U.S. Department of Justice announced a Los Angeles health care fraud takedown totaling nearly $2 billion. Fifteen people were charged, and six of them are medical professionals.

## The biggest case

The largest piece of the takedown is a $1.6 billion scheme that allegedly lured vulnerable people into sham rehab programs. DOJ calls it the largest case of its kind it has ever prosecuted.
