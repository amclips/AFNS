**Status: Allegations; indictment.** An indictment is a formal accusation, not a finding of guilt. **The defendants are presumed innocent** unless proven guilty in court.

## The indictment

On Oct. 28, the U.S. Attorney's Office in Mobile unsealed an indictment charging six people connected to JB Accounting Tax Service LLC ([Atlanta News First](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2026/10/29/indictment-6-accused-filing-9000-fraudulent-tax-returns-seeking-177-million-refunds/); [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/fraud/fraud-enforcement-news)).

- **The defendants:** owner Jennifer Beverly, 47, and employees Kennedy Lawrence Stovall Winston, Jadasha Bowman, Solida Lucille Green, Paris Lovett and Shermeka Benjamin.
- **The charges:** wire-fraud conspiracy and wire fraud.
- **Winston:** the former University of Alabama basketball player, 42, also faces a separate federal charge over stolen Treasury checks ([FOX10](https://www.fox10tv.com/2026/10/28/mobile-tax-preparer-former-alabama-basketball-star-among-those-indicted-177-million-fraud-case/); Atlanta News First).

## What the indictment alleges

The indictment says the group filed more than 9,000 false returns from 2021 to 2024, claiming $177 million in refunds. It says the IRS paid about $81 million of that. The alleged false claims include COVID-19 credits, fuel tax credits and education credits (Atlanta News First).

## The investigation

The case is part of an operation by the DOJ's National Fraud Enforcement Division, IRS Criminal Investigation and the FBI. The DOJ listed it on Oct. 28 (DOJ).

## What's next

Each defendant faces up to 20 years in prison if convicted. No court date is set yet (Atlanta News First).
