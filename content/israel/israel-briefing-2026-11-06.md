## Netanyahu office: from aid to partnership

Netanyahu's office framed Trump's plan to end roughly $4 billion a year in U.S. military aid as a shift **"from aid to partnership"** and mutual investment, saying Israel can still buy U.S. weapons and could get extra help if needed ([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-914053)). Full story: [Report: Trump Says U.S. Military Aid to Israel Will End This Year](/israel/report-trump-says-us-military-aid-israel-will-end-this-year/).

## Iran: no U.S. buildup seen; IRGC claims UNVERIFIED

Ynet, citing unnamed defense officials as relayed in regional coverage, says Israel sees **no major U.S. buildup** for renewed strikes on Iran while the IDF refines plans that could include another round against Hezbollah ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611068872), [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/morning-update-662), [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)). **UNVERIFIED:** Iranian state sources / the IRGC claim two U.S. drones were downed over Qeshm and that tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz; those claims are not independently confirmed.

## Gaza, Lebanon, Syria

Local sources report Israeli strikes continued in Gaza, Lebanon and Syria. Figures for Palestinians killed in the past day **vary by source from at least 4 to 7**; AFNS reports the range rather than a single number ([Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/israeli-forces-strike-targets-syria-and-lebanon-simultaneously), [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)). Coverage also notes Israeli strikes at al-Tayri in Bint Jbeil district, Lebanon, and targets in southern Syria.

## Quick hits

- The UN condemned a settler takeover of Palestinian homes near Jericho that displaced dozens, and reported attacks on olive pickers ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)).
- Members of the European Parliament urged a binding EU ban on trade in settlement products ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)).
- Houthis fired missiles toward Madinah and Najran in Saudi Arabia; the Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted them ([Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/morning-update-662)).
