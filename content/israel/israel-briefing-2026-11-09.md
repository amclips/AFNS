## Report: ~250 foreign troops for Gaza ISF

Walla, citing security officials (Jerusalem Post, Jewish Chronicle): Israel approved an initial U.S.-led deployment of about **250 foreign troops** for a Gaza stabilization force. Willing countries listed; Israel opposes Turkey; **no arrival date**. Full story: [Report: Israel Approves First 250 Foreign Troops for Gaza Stabilization Force](/israel/report-israel-approves-first-250-foreign-troops-gaza-stabilization-force/).

## Russian GRU chief visit (Jerusalem Post)

Two sources told the [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-914291) that a Russian military transport carrying a delegation led by **Adm. Igor Kostyukov**, chief of Russia's GRU military intelligence, landed at Ben-Gurion Airport and met senior Israeli defense officials. The purpose of the meetings was **not disclosed**. The aircraft later left for Abu Dhabi, where the delegation is due at U.S.-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks. Ynet separately reported the landing of a U.S.-sanctioned Russian Defense Ministry aircraft without naming passengers ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rjneow1rgx)). The IDF and Russian Embassy declined comment to JPost.

## Gaza — embankment collapse

The Hamas-run health ministry and Reuters/BBC reporting say **four people** — three children and one woman — were killed when a **sand embankment collapsed onto tents** at a displacement camp south of Deir al-Balah after heavy rain ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqd090ee8px5o), [Al-Monitor / Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/four-killed-gaza-after-sand-embankment-collapses-tents)).
