**Status: The IDF said Tuesday it had received reports of arsons in the Palestinian village of Luban a-Sharqiya, also known as al-Lubban ash-Sharqiya, and that it was searching for suspects, [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/gornlpqfk) reported.** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/10/massive-fire-as-israeli-settlers-burn-down-business-near-nablus) reported that Israeli settlers set fire to a mechanic workshop and vehicle scrapyard in the village, near Nablus. The IDF has not publicly identified who started the fires.

An IDF spokesperson, quoted by Ynet, said security forces went to the village after reports of arsons and after footage from the scene showed vehicles on fire. "Forces are currently operating in the area to locate suspects and restore order," the spokesperson said, according to Ynet. "Upon completion of firefighting operations, police forces and forensic investigators will enter under IDF force protection to collect findings and evidence."

Al Jazeera, in a Nov. 10 video report, said Israeli settlers set fire to Palestinian property in Luban ash-Sharqiya and that residents filmed firefighters trying to contain a blaze that tore through a mechanic workshop and vehicle scrapyard. That is Al Jazeera's account. It has not been confirmed by the IDF statement reported by Ynet, which described reports of arsons and an ongoing search for suspects.
