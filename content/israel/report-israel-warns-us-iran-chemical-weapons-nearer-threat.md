**Status: Reported by the New York Times; officials divided.** This story rests on the Times' reporting, which cites unnamed U.S. and regional officials, along with the Jerusalem Post, Times of Israel and Ynet accounts of it. AFNS has not seen the underlying intelligence. The Israeli military declined to comment, according to the Times.

## What the Times reports

Israel has shared intelligence with the United States asserting that Iran is considering the use of chemical weapons against its adversaries, the New York Times reports, citing U.S. and regional officials ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/03/world/middleeast/iran-chemical-weapons-israel-united-states-trump.html)). The report first circulated widely on X via [Polymarket](https://x.com/i/status/2117678781598990784).

In conversations with U.S. spy agencies and other officials over the past two weeks, Israeli officials have portrayed the chemical weapons threat as more urgent than Iran's ambitions to develop a nuclear weapon, according to the Times. Israeli intelligence told U.S. counterparts it believes Iran is moving rapidly to develop the technology to put such weapons on a missile or smuggle them into Israel or another country, according to American officials cited by the Times.

## Officials divided

The Times reports that the intelligence was met with alarm by some American officials and deep skepticism by others.

- **Skeptics:** some U.S. officials see the intelligence as part of a pressure campaign by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's government to persuade the Trump administration to return to major combat operations against Iran.
- **Gulf officials:** Israel shared some of the information with Gulf countries. Several Gulf officials doubted Iran was developing such plans and believe Israel is trying to push the U.S. and its partners back to a more intense phase of the war. A regional official said Israel had not shared supporting evidence (as relayed by the [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913753) and [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/hjm05jpafx)).
- **Those taking it seriously:** other officials say the threat should be taken seriously given recent warnings about Iranian preparations to attack American interests.

Background, per the Times: Iran acknowledged in 2003 that it developed chemical capabilities during the Iran-Iraq war and said it later dismantled them. U.S. annual intelligence assessments say Iran "likely aims" to continue chemical weapons research and development.

The Times also reports that Israel's Home Front Command drills this week are not focusing on chemical weapons preparation ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/nyt-israel-tells-skeptical-us-gulf-countries-that-iran-could-use-chemical-weapons-against-adversaries/)).

## UNVERIFIED: CENTCOM strike options

An X story summary says CENTCOM has strike options ready as early as Nov. 4 ([X](https://x.com/i/trending/2117682098093924403)). The Jerusalem Post, in its account of the Times report, says senior U.S. officials described a CENTCOM plan that could be carried out as early as Wednesday, Nov. 4. AFNS has not independently verified this. **There has been no go-ahead from President Trump.**
