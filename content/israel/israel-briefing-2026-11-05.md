## Ra'am backs Trump's Gaza plan

Mansour Abbas told President Herzog that Ra'am fully supports Trump's Gaza peace plan and disarming Hamas, and urged quick formation of a government ([Jerusalem Post live updates](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/2026-11-05/live-updates-913880)). Otzma Yehudit demanded that every party condemn Hamas before recommending a candidate. With 19 Arab-party seats, whether Eisenkot relies on them remains the central coalition question.

## Fallout from Trump's plan to end U.S. military aid

Trump says roughly $4 billion a year in U.S. military aid to Israel stops after this year, that arms sales continue, and that Netanyahu agreed ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/middle-east/trump-says-us-will-stop-paying-israel-after-year-end-2026-11-03/), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/trump-israel-military-aid-hardware-5cad93330300b2d45a9665d5eccb2ab2), [NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/11/04/us-news/us-to-withdraw-4b-in-israel-aid-as-trump-weighs-next-move-in-iran-war/)). The current agreement runs to September 2028, so AP and Reuters say an abrupt cutoff is unlikely without Congress. A U.S. official told the NY Post the phase-down was negotiated for months and could begin in October 2027. Full story: [Report: Trump Says U.S. Military Aid to Israel Will End This Year](/israel/report-trump-says-us-military-aid-israel-will-end-this-year/).

## Iran: quiet small strikes while talks drag on

The Wall Street Journal has reported that the U.S. carried out small, unpublicized airstrikes on Iran, including on Qeshm Island on Nov. 2 ([JINSA update](https://jinsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Iran-War-Update-11.4.26.pdf), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-news-november-3-2026-9ae5cec3d1f9cde02652f52f7f80fbce)). Trump told the Financial Times he has no plans to strike Iran because "we seem to be getting everything we want." Qatar is still mediating; Iran wants China to guarantee any deal. U.S. officials remain skeptical of Israel's chemical-weapons warning. Related: [U.S. Officials: Limited 'Defensive' Strikes Hit Iranian Missiles on Qeshm Island](/israel/report-us-strikes-iranian-missile-launcher-qeshm-island/).

## Quick hits

- **Lebanon/Gaza:** Kan says Israel asked Lebanon, via U.S. mediators, to deploy troops and checkpoints in Hezbollah-held areas. An Israeli drone strike in southern Lebanon killed one; Gaza strikes killed at least two ([JINSA](https://jinsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Iran-War-Update-11.4.26.pdf)). See also [Israeli Drone Strike Near Qabrikha in Southern Lebanon Kills One](/israel/israeli-drone-strike-qabrikha-southern-lebanon-kills-paramedic/).
- **Election funding claims:** Election officials reject Netanyahu's claim that Qatar "stole" the election by funding Arab turnout; an investigation is open but would not invalidate the result. Former Joint List leader Ayman Odeh is **suspected** of receiving foreign funds, according to coverage of the probe ([American Israelite](https://americanisraelite.com/israel-briefs-november-5-2026/), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/2026-11-05/live-updates-913880)).
- **Hebron:** The IDF ended an 11-day Hebron operation that followed a deadly checkpoint shooting ([Times of Israel daily briefing](https://www.timesofisrael.com/daily-briefing-nov-5-trumps-bombshell-end-of-israel-military-aid-good-for-the-jewish-state/)).
- **Other:** Beilinson Hospital performed Israel's first robotic lung transplant, and the Defense Ministry signed a $170 million deal with Elbit for an artillery training center ([American Israelite](https://americanisraelite.com/israel-briefs-november-5-2026/)).
