## Update, Nov. 5, 5 PM CT

President Trump spoke to reporters in the **Oval Office** Thursday afternoon. Per [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202610312594) and [Al Jazeera's live blog](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/5/iran-war-live-trump-admits-war-caused-election-loss-but-had-to-do-it) (also relayed by [Barak Ravid](https://x.com/BarakRavid/status/2118455756445962685) and [i24NEWS](https://x.com/i24NEWS_EN/status/2118460815355343164) on X), he said:

> I would rather not destroy the rest of the country if possible, but maybe I will have to. You will know soon enough.

He said he thinks it will take Iran **25 years** to rebuild, claimed the United States already controls "everything we wanted to control," and added that "a lot of things [are] cooking" over the next week or two. Those lines are **sharper** than his morning/election-night "maybe I will, maybe I won't" framing. **He has not announced an order** to resume major combat.

### Iran nuclear proposal via Qatar (Reuters)

A senior Iranian official told [Reuters](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-914025) (also [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-914025)) that Tehran has sent the United States a **nuclear proposal through Qatar**, to be discussed only after Washington meets Tehran's **seven demands**, which include sanctions relief, release of frozen assets, and ending the naval blockade, among other conditions. The official said the U.S. was reviewing the proposal. Asked about it, Trump told reporters Iran is "not doing anything without our approval" and "they're not going to have a nuclear weapon."


**Status: Reported; not confirmed by the White House.**

The [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/05/us/politics/trump-iran-war-midterm-elections.html) reported on **Nov. 5, 2026** — citing unnamed Pentagon officials and military commanders who spoke **Wednesday** — that thousands of American air and naval forces on more than a dozen ships and at bases around the Middle East and in the United States are poised to resume major combat operations against Iran if President Donald Trump orders it. [DNYUZ](https://dnyuz.com/2026/11/05/political-losses-deepen-the-stakes-as-trump-contemplates-renewed-iran-attack/) and [JFeed](https://www.jfeed.com/news/us-forces-ready-iran-combat-trump-order) carry the same account. Officials, speaking on condition of anonymity to discuss operational matters, said the U.S. and Israeli militaries have drawn up plans for a short, intensive campaign whose likely targets include energy infrastructure, nuclear facilities, and missile and drone arsenals.

[Al Jazeera's Nov. 5 live blog](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/5/iran-war-live-trump-admits-war-caused-election-loss-but-had-to-do-it) relays the Times report and says Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi held separate calls with his counterparts in Saudi Arabia, Oman and Qatar amid mounting fears of a U.S. attack.

## Trump's conflicting signals

On election night **Nov. 4**, Trump told Fox News (Martha MacCallum and Bret Baier) that the United States had "wiped them out, practically," but had not "finished the job," adding: "maybe I will, maybe I won't" ([Mediaite / Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/fox-news-anchor-presses-trump-051901563.html), [Fox News live](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/iran-war-news-trump-elections-oil-prices-november-4)). That sits alongside his earlier Financial Times interview remark that the United States has no immediate plans to strike Iran because "we seem to be getting everything we want."

**No White House confirmation** of a decision to resume major combat has been issued. Limited U.S. strikes on Iranian missiles around Qeshm Island earlier this week were described by U.S. officials as defensive, not a return to major operations.
