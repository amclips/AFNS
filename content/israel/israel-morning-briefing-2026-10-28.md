## UN inquiry accuses Israel; Israel and the U.S. reject the findings

**Based on an X news summary; not independently verified.** AFNS has not reviewed the report itself.

According to the X news summary:

- **The report:** the UN Commission of Inquiry's fifth report was released Oct. 26.
- **The accusation:** it accuses Israel of war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza and the West Bank since October 2023.
- **Gaza:** it alleges "ongoing genocidal acts" in Gaza, even after the October 2025 ceasefire.
- **West Bank:** it says more than 34,000 West Bank Palestinians have been displaced.
- **Response:** Israel and the United States rejected the findings.

These are the commission's accusations, not established findings of any court.

## Quick hits

- **Lebanon:** Lebanon's national news agency, NNA, says Israeli artillery shelled Majdal Zoun and Haddatha in southern Lebanon on Wednesday morning (via Pakistan TV's live coverage).
- **Gaza:** the UN says about 950,000 displaced people live in sites at risk of flooding as Gaza's fourth winter in tents begins. It also says flour shortages briefly shut most WFP-backed bakeries last week (UN News; OCHA).
- **West Bank (based on an X news summary; not independently verified):** Israeli forces demolished Palestinian memorials in Nablus-area villages on Oct. 27, according to the summary. The IDF calls it counterterrorism work.
- **Arab turnout (based on an X news summary; not independently verified):** about 80% of voters in Arab communities turned out, the highest in decades, according to the summary.
- **Ben-Gvir (based on an X news summary; not independently verified):** according to the summary, Ben-Gvir again called for conquering Gaza and urged Palestinians to emigrate to Europe. His remarks drew wide backlash.
