The [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/eb4d1101-45ed-4d03-9beb-42f7c87a58ea) reports that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has been **pressing President Trump almost daily** to **resume large-scale U.S.-Israeli strikes** on Iran, and that Netanyahu **ordered the IDF to plan a major new offensive** earlier this month. The account relies on **anonymous sources**. **Israel has not publicly confirmed** the FT reporting. Secondary coverage of the FT piece includes [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/sjoxipkrzx) and [Yeshiva World](https://www.theyeshivaworld.com/news/israel-news/2618232/pressing-for-action-netanyahu-urges-trump-to-resume-iran-war-right-after-midterms.html).

A diplomat briefed on the discussions told the FT that Netanyahu calls Trump **"every day"** to urge him to renew the war, that people inside and outside the White House who oppose escalation are trying to block that, and that **regional leaders** are telling Trump **not to escalate** and to prioritize talks ([Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/sjoxipkrzx) summarizing FT).

A **U.S. official** confirmed that the leaders spoke and that Netanyahu wants hostilities to resume, but said Trump is **"not there yet."** A person familiar with the matter said the United States is giving diplomacy **"a last-ditch chance"** to succeed ([Yeshiva World](https://www.theyeshivaworld.com/news/israel-news/2618232/pressing-for-action-netanyahu-urges-trump-to-resume-iran-war-right-after-midterms.html) citing FT).

## Qatar-led interim outline (per FT)

The FT account also describes a **Qatar-led** interim outline under which Iran would reopen the **Strait of Hormuz** in exchange for the United States lifting its blockade on Iranian ports, allowing oil exports, and releasing access to **some frozen assets**, then returning to talks on a longer agreement.
