## Update, Nov. 9 (evening)

**Haredi talks.** [Kan](https://www.kan.org.il/content/kan-news/politic/1117627/), as reported by the [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/report-eisenkot-liberman-agree-to-open-talks-with-haredi-parties-eyeing-draft-law-concessions/), says Gadi Eisenkot and Avigdor Liberman agreed to **open talks** with **Shas and United Torah Judaism**, with a **softened draft law** on offer for support of an Eisenkot-led government. **Channel 12** says discussions are still at an **early stage**. Per that reporting, Haredi parties are expected to seek a **one-year to 18-month** transitional arrangement with **no meaningful enlistment, sanctions, or arrests** of draft dodgers in that period, plus **legal guarantees** that the arrangement would withstand court review.

**Speakership.** [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/syteack0fe) reports UTJ proposed interim speakership for chairman **Yaakov Asher** to help guarantee a draft-law vote; Liberman and Eisenkot later agreed the speakership goes to a **Yashar** member instead. UTJ **refused to sign** a letter seeking to replace Speaker **Amir Ohana** before Tuesday's swearing-in, saying the speaker should change alongside forming a new government. **Shas**, for now, **remains aligned with Netanyahu**.

**Avoiding Arab-party dependence.** The [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/11/09/israeli-opposition-eyes-deal-with-ultra-orthodox-jewish-parties-oust-netanyahu/) reports Eisenkot is pursuing ultra-Orthodox options in part to **avoid a partnership dependent on Arab parties**.

**West Bank — Beita industrial zone.** The [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/settlers-reported-to-have-set-vehicles-on-fire-in-northern-west-bank/) and [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/west-bank/2026-11-09/ty-article/.premium/settlers-set-palestinian-vehicles-ablaze-near-west-banks-nablus-reports-say/000001a1-c16c-d0f7-adfb-f56d759c0000), citing Palestinian media, report settlers set fire to excavators, vehicles, and equipment in an industrial area near **Beita**, south of Nablus. No injuries were immediately reported.

## Update, Nov. 9 (afternoon)

**Eshel and Abbas.** The [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-914380), citing N12, reports that Netanyahu confidant **Natan Eshel** repeatedly contacted Ra'am chairman **Mansour Abbas** before, during, and after the election about whether Abbas would back **Gadi Eisenkot**. Likud told N12 that Netanyahu was **surprised** and that the contacts were **not** done with his knowledge. Eshel gave N12 shifting accounts of the exchanges.

**Shin Bet meddling claims.** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/israels-shin-bet-finds-no-evidence-of-foreign-election-meddling-report), citing [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-11-09/ty-article/.premium/shin-bet-yet-to-foreign-election-interference-evidence-officials-tell-haaretz/000001a1-bf56-dc4a-a7a9-bff7b14a0000), reports that senior law-enforcement officials say the Shin Bet investigation has **found no evidence** of foreign election meddling, despite claims by Prime Minister Netanyahu and Shin Bet chief David Zini. The probe continues. Qatar has denied the allegations. Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara said she had seen no evidence that would have changed the election results.

**Swearing-in.** The **26th Knesset** is due to be sworn in on **Tuesday, Nov. 10**. Coverage notes **47 first-time MKs** among the 120 members ([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-914382), [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/byqwj18tmx)).

## Update, Nov. 9 — Netanyahu unity offer

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu released a video Monday calling a national unity government **"simply an absolute necessity"** and saying **"Enough with the boycotts"** ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/hjs8ptjcfx), [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-914356)). He said Likud should lead because it is the **largest party**, that the government must include the **Haredi** parties, and that he rejects a government that depends on Arab parties — characterizing them as **"Hamas supporters"** (his words; AFNS attributes that label to him, not as fact). **No rotation** was mentioned.

Eisenkot is **starting coalition talks**. As of Monday he has **27 days** left on the mandate clock that started Nov. 8 ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/hjs8ptjcfx)).

**Jerusalem Post** reports that Avigdor Liberman and Gadi Eisenkot **agreed Monday on the next Knesset speaker**. The **name has not been reported**. After their meeting, both said they are **confident they can form a government** for a full term ([Jerusalem Post live updates](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/2026-11-09/live-updates-914287)).

Separately, Eisenkot's Yashar party is **courting Shas and United Torah Judaism** to join an opposition-bloc push to **replace Knesset Speaker Amir Ohana** when the 26th Knesset is sworn in. UTJ chairman Yaakov Asher said the party will not support replacing the speaker before a new government is formed. **Shas is waiting on UTJ** before moving toward Eisenkot, per Jerusalem Post reporting ([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-914332); [live updates](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/2026-11-09/live-updates-914287)).

## Mandate (Sunday, Nov. 8)

President Isaac Herzog **gave Eisenkot the mandate** to form the next government on **Sunday, Nov. 8** ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/07/g-s1-152536/netanyahu-israel-government-eisenkot-herzog), [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-gets-nod-to-form-government-vows-unconventional-acts-in-time-of-emergency/)). The recommendation tally was **67 for Eisenkot and 46 for Netanyahu**. Eisenkot has **28 days**, with a possible **14-day extension**. The clock **started Nov. 8**.

**Reminder:** Recommendations are not a governing coalition. As of Friday, only about **48 MKs** in Eisenkot's own camp were reported ready to govern together; the **19 Arab-party** recommendations were not a coalition commitment ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-bolstered-by-pm-recommendations-but-still-well-short-of-a-government/), via prior AFNS coverage). No mainstream update Monday overturned that 48-MK nuance.

## Handala leaflets (labeled)

**Police and Shin Bet** are investigating leaflets found Monday in **northern Tel Aviv** mailboxes with QR codes linking to a page tied to the Iran-linked **Handala** hacker group, which claimed responsibility and threatened Israeli energy and water infrastructure.
