**Published Nov. 2, 2026, 11 PM CT.** Covers reports from about 3 PM to 11 PM CT Monday.

**Status: Reported; not officially confirmed.** The first report of explosions came from Iranian state media. The U.S. account comes from the Wall Street Journal, citing U.S. officials, and from Axios, citing a single U.S. official. No official CENTCOM or Pentagon statement is cited in the reports.

## Explosions reported on Qeshm Island

Around 2:50 PM CT Monday, Iran's state news agency IRNA reported five explosions on Qeshm Island, Iran's largest island, in the Strait of Hormuz (via [Faytuks on X](https://x.com/Faytuks/status/2117361704271032424)). Residents said they saw aircraft overhead (via [Faytuks on X](https://x.com/Faytuks/status/2117359309067599961)). This is Iranian state media's account.

## WSJ: one of a series of limited U.S. strikes

The Wall Street Journal, citing U.S. officials, reports that the strike was one of a series of limited, unannounced U.S. strikes on Iran in recent weeks, some of which officials described as defensive (via [Iran International on X](https://x.com/IranIntl_En/status/2117424532718022745) and [Sentdefender on X](https://x.com/sentdefender/status/2117433984263004443)).

## SINGLE SOURCE: Axios on a missile launcher

Axios, citing one U.S. official, reports that a U.S. fighter jet destroyed an IRGC surface-to-air missile launcher on Qeshm in self-defense, after the IRGC fired a missile at a U.S. aircraft over the Strait of Hormuz (via [Iran International on X](https://x.com/IranIntl_En/status/2117439277139169321)). This detail rests on a single unnamed official and has not been independently confirmed.

## Oil markets

Brent crude rose about 0.5% in early Asian trading after the reports (via [Iran International on X](https://x.com/IranIntl_En/status/2117451623324877012)).

## Trump on Pickaxe Mountain

President Trump told Fox News the U.S. "might have to go after" Pickaxe Mountain, a site tied to Iran's nuclear program, and warned that fighting could resume if Tehran does not reach a deal (via [Iran International on X](https://x.com/IranIntl_En/status/2117464401578176905)).

## China and Oman on Hormuz

During the Sultan of Oman's state visit to Beijing, a China-Oman joint statement called for safe passage through the Strait of Hormuz and urged the U.S. and Iran to keep negotiating (via [X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2116913936968626268)).

## What's next

- No official CENTCOM or Pentagon statement on the Qeshm strike is cited in the reports.
- Israel's official election results go to President Isaac Herzog on Nov. 4. There is no new vote count or coalition news.
