**Report.** Security officials told [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/military/383960472) that the Israeli government has authorized the United States to deploy an **initial multinational force of about 250 foreign troops** in Gaza as part of preparations for an International Stabilization Force, the [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-914289) and [Jewish Chronicle](https://www.thejc.com/news/israel/israel-approves-foreign-troops-gaza-peace-plan-ufnb7gzz) report.

Countries that have expressed willingness to participate, per that reporting: **Indonesia, Morocco, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Uganda, and Albania**. Kazakhstan offered medical and logistical help; Kosovo offered security and policing support.

**Israel opposes Turkish involvement.** Walla/JPost say Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan has pressed Washington to include Turkey; officials in Jerusalem and the defense establishment reject that.

Foreign military officers accompanied by U.S. troops have been seen near the Gaza border in recent weeks as preparations advance. **No arrival date** was reported.
