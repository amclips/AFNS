## Update, Nov. 4, 3 PM CT

Israel's high security alert remains in place amid concern that Iran could launch a preemptive strike, a senior Israeli security official told i24NEWS on Wednesday, according to [Breaking The News](https://www.breakingthenews.net/Article/Israel-said-to-prepare-for-preemptive-Iranian-strike/67485451), which relayed the report. The official said Tehran does not want to be "caught off guard for a third time" while it waits on Washington's decision about further military action.

X posts relaying i24NEWS and Israel's Channel 15 ([1](https://x.com/11Sundesha/status/2118083486762582123), [2](https://x.com/DC_Global_News/status/2118086887286116767)) say U.S. transport planes carrying air-defense and reconnaissance assets are arriving in Israel. AFNS could not find either outlet's own report of the arrivals. Neither the U.S. nor the Israeli government has confirmed what the flights are carrying, so this story's not-officially-confirmed label stays.

**Status: Not officially confirmed; awaiting confirmation.** Neither the U.S. nor the Israeli government has said what the aircraft are carrying.

An [X news summary](https://x.com/i/trending/2117725065030803485) says flight data shows U.S. Air Force planes delivering THAAD and Patriot systems to Israeli airfields as Israel steps up emergency preparations over a possible escalation with Iran.

## What the flight data shows

Open-source trackers have logged at least 16 U.S. Air Force C-17 flights into Israel's Ovda Air Base since mid-October, 11 of them from Fort Bliss, Texas, home to U.S. Army Patriot and THAAD units, according to [Defence Blog](https://defence-blog.com/u-s-air-force-steps-up-c-17-airlift-to-israel-and-kuwait/). [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/-us-military-airlift-activity-suggests-possible-additional-patriot-deployment-to-israel/4110546) says the scale of the airlift could point to a full Patriot battery, but that this has not been independently confirmed.

The Wall Street Journal reported on Oct. 27, citing two officials and flight-tracking data, that a Patriot system was being sent from Texas to Israel (as relayed by [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/us/2026-10-28/iran-patriot-israel-strikes-build-up-us-23288005.html)). Open-source trackers say some shipments may involve THAAD. Flight data cannot show what is on board.

## What officials say

U.S. Central Command says it does not discuss such movements for operational security reasons and "regularly rotates and repositions U.S. forces and assets," Stars and Stripes reports.
