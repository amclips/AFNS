**Status: Reported; attacker's age unclear.** This account comes from the Serbian government, BBC and AP. AFNS is not naming the attacker.

## What happened

Just after midnight local time, a man armed with two knives attacked a Serbian police officer guarding Israel's embassy in Belgrade. The attacker shouted "Allahu Akbar" (BBC; AP).

- **The officer** suffered a minor cut to his face and shot the attacker dead.
- **Prosecutors** ruled the shooting self-defense.

## Serbia's response

- **Terrorism:** Serbian authorities called it a terrorist attack (Government of Serbia).
- **The suspect:** officials said he was a known Islamic State supporter who was under surveillance.
- **Threat level:** Serbia raised its national terror threat level to red, the highest.

## Israel's response

Israel's foreign ministry said no embassy staff were hurt because the embassy was closed at the time (BBC; AP).

## Conflicting reports on the attacker's age

Reports conflict on how old the attacker was. Officials have said he was a minor, but emergency services told the Beta news agency he was 24 (as reported by BBC and AP). AFNS is not naming him.

## Second attack since June 2024

This is the second attack on Israel's embassy in Belgrade since June 2024 (AP; BBC).
