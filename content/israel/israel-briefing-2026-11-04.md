## Hormuz: IRGC tanker claims

**UNVERIFIED:** the IRGC claims that four tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz and were destroyed, and that it stopped two more ships. CENTCOM has rejected similar claims before. Separately, UKMTO reported two projectiles hitting a tanker off Oman ([IranWire](https://iranwire.com/en/news/159779-irgc-claims-four-tankers-destroyed-by-mines-in-strait-of-hormuz/)).

## Lebanon: keeping Hezbollah out

Kan reports that Israel asked Beirut, through U.S. mediators, to deploy troops and checkpoints to keep Hezbollah out of any renewed war with Iran (as relayed by the [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-said-pressing-lebanon-to-prevent-hezbollah-from-joining-renewed-war-with-iran/)). Asharq Al-Awsat reports Secretary of State Marco Rubio warned that Israel won't stand idle if Hezbollah joins (as relayed by [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/rubio-warning-of-israeli-escalation-if-hezbollah-backs-iran-says-report-3009620)). Israeli shelling continues around Nabatieh ([L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1553093/israeli-artillery-fire-across-occupied-zone-multiple-fires-reported-in-nabatieh-region.html)).

## Quick hits

- **Gaza:** The IDF and Shin Bet say strikes in Khan Younis on Sunday and Monday killed six operatives, including a Nukhba company commander ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-4-2026/)).
- **West Bank:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/israeli-forces-arrest-al-jazeera-journalist-in-raid-on-his-west-bank-home) reports raids in Dura, near Hebron, detained about 20 people, including its journalist Montaser Nassar; all were later released. There were also arrests in Tubas, Qalqilya and Nablus. Palestinian media report a new land seizure order near Ramallah for a settlement road ([Al-Quds](https://alquds.com/en/posts/268853-widespread-arrest-campaign-in-the-west-bank-and-jerusalem-and-the-occupation)).
- **Rabin:** Today marks 31 years since the assassination of Yitzhak Rabin, which Herzog noted ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/full-text-of-president-herzogs-remarks-on-receiving-official-2026-election-results/)).
