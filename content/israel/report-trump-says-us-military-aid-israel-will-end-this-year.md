**Status: Reported by Haaretz; no response from Netanyahu's office.** This story rests on Haaretz's report, as posted on X. AFNS has not seen an official U.S. or Israeli statement confirming the details.

## What Haaretz reports

President Trump said the roughly $4 billion a year in direct U.S. military aid to Israel will end by the end of this year, Haaretz reports ([Haaretz on X](https://x.com/haaretzcom/status/2117620728065675490); see also [Real America's Voice on X](https://x.com/RealAmVoice/status/2117616998301467092)).

According to Haaretz, Trump said he and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu agreed to it. "He's very understanding of it," Trump said, as reported by Haaretz.

Netanyahu's office had not responded as of the report.

## What the reporting does not say

Viral posts claim the U.S. will end **all** assistance to Israel. The reporting does not back that up. Haaretz's report concerns direct U.S. military aid of roughly $4 billion a year.
