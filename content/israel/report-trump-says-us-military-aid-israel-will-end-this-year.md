## Update, Nov. 9 — Conflicting accounts

**Conflicting accounts** of how and when U.S. military financing for Israel ends:

**Rubio (Jerusalem Post):** Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Israel is now **"prosperous and rich enough"** to buy U.S. weapons without U.S. financing, that **"it's something the Israelis themselves have offered us,"** and that a **2027** start matches **"what the president said we're going to do"** ([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-914383)).

**Ynet:** An emerging security memorandum would **not** end aid this year. Under Ynet's account, current assistance runs through **2028**, then roughly **$3.3 billion** a year in Foreign Military Financing would continue for **five years** (about **$16.5 billion** total) before direct military aid ends — roughly **halving** a prior 10-year-style package total. Joint missile-defense funding may rise. Negotiators aim to finalize before the new Congress in January ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bjqg7k1cml)).

AFNS presents both accounts side by side. They are **not** reconciled in public reporting reviewed for this update.

## Update, Nov. 9

On CBS **60 Minutes** (interview aired Sunday, Nov. 8), President Trump said countries the United States protected in the Iran war **"are going to pay us back any and all costs that we've had for the war"** because **"without us, they wouldn't exist. Without us, Israel would not exist."** He added that **"Israel would not exist except for Donald Trump"** and credited B-2 strikes on Iranian nuclear sites. The interview **does not state an amount**, a repayment schedule, or evidence of a signed agreement. Quotes verified against the [CBS 60 Minutes transcript](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/donald-trump-2026-post-midterm-elections-interview-60-minutes-transcript/).

On the midterms, Trump said voters **"didn't like the war,"** **"didn't like the fact that gasoline went up,"** and that there is **"a great dislike, incredibly, for Israel."** Asked whether he would escalate the Iran war now that the midterms are over, he said: **"Well, I'm not looking to. I don't think there's any reason to."**

**Separately** (not the 60 Minutes sit-down), Trump told reporters that Gadi Eisenkot and Benjamin Netanyahu are **"very similar in terms of their policy and their view"** and **"have very similar views"** ([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-914279), [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rkwhmcrtgx), [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/trump-says-eisenkot-and-netanyahu-are-very-similar-in-terms-of-their-policy/)).

## Update, Nov. 6

Netanyahu's office gave its first response to Trump's plan to end roughly $4 billion a year in U.S. military aid, framing it as a shift **"from aid to partnership"** and mutual investment, the [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-914053) and an [Israeli embassy newsletter](https://embassies.gov.il/sites/default/files/2026-11/6.11.26_embassy_of_israel_newsletter.pdf) report. The PMO says Netanyahu has been working with Trump on the transition, that Israel can still **buy U.S. weapons**, and that Israel could get extra help if needed.

## Update, Nov. 4

[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/middle-east/trump-says-us-will-stop-paying-israel-after-year-end-2026-11-03/) reports that on Election Day, President Trump said he had agreed with Prime Minister Netanyahu to end the aid after 2026 while weapons sales continue, and that Israel no longer needs U.S. financial support. Reuters notes that the current agreement, known as an MOU, provides $3.8 billion a year through 2028; Haaretz and others cited roughly $4 billion. Reuters says Trump framed the move partly as removing a "bad talking point" that left-wing Democrats had been using.

## Update, Nov. 3, 11 AM CT

**Weapons sales to continue.** Trump told Fox News on Tuesday that Israel will still be able to buy U.S. weapons even as the roughly $4 billion a year in direct aid ends. "They can buy whatever they want," he said ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-says-israel-stop-receiving-4b-us-aid-years-end-can-still-buy-weapons)). AP reports Trump said the U.S. will stop the aid in 2027 but will sell arms to Israel ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/trump-israel-military-aid-hardware-5cad93330300b2d45a9665d5eccb2ab2)).

## What Haaretz reports

President Trump said the roughly $4 billion a year in direct U.S. military aid to Israel will end by the end of this year, Haaretz reports ([Haaretz on X](https://x.com/haaretzcom/status/2117620728065675490); see also [Real America's Voice on X](https://x.com/RealAmVoice/status/2117616998301467092)).

According to Haaretz, Trump said he and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu agreed to it. "He's very understanding of it," Trump said, as reported by Haaretz.

Netanyahu's office had not responded as of the report.

## What the reporting does not say

Viral posts claim the U.S. will end **all** assistance to Israel. The reporting does not back that up. Haaretz's report concerns direct U.S. military aid of roughly $4 billion a year.
