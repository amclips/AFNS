**Fact check: misleading framing.** The quote is real commentary by a think-tank figure; it is not an official Chinese government warning.

Posts circulating on X claim China warned Israel that a nuclear strike on Iran would mean the end of Israel. An [X news summary](https://x.com/i/trending/2117668004918178285) says the quote comes from a resurfaced clip of Victor Gao, a Beijing think-tank commentator, and is not official Chinese policy.

## Who Victor Gao is

Gao is vice president of the Center for China and Globalization, a Beijing think tank, and a former diplomat, according to [IARI](https://english.iari.site/did-china-really-threaten-israels-end/) and [VerifyMate](https://verifymate.ai/v/FlRSxZRGdxwzI6XZmdBBpg). In the clip, he says the moment Israel uses a nuclear warhead against any country, including Iran, "it will be the end of Israel as a country, as a state." Both say he is not a spokesperson for the Chinese government.

## What Beijing has said officially

IARI found no equivalent wording in official Chinese channels. It says Beijing's official statements have condemned the U.S.-Israeli attacks on Iran, called for a halt to military operations and urged a return to negotiations. Some outlets have run the line as a statement by "China," for example [The Defender](https://thedefenderngr.com/if-israel-uses-nuclear-weapon-on-iran-israel-will-cease-to-exist-says-china/); VerifyMate rates that framing misleading. AFNS could not establish when the clip was first recorded.
