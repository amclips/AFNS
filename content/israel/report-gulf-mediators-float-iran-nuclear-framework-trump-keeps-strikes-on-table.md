## Update, Nov. 9 (evening)

[Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/09/trump-iran-war-qatar-negotiations-attacks-unlikely) (Barak Ravid): a diplomat briefed on Trump's meeting with Qatar's prime minister said Trump **wants a deal** and **will give negotiations a chance**, while noting he could reverse course if Iran is not serious.

**Status: UNCONFIRMED** for the enrichment framework. **No official confirmation** from the United States, Qatar, Pakistan, or Iran.

## UNCONFIRMED: Qatar-Pakistan framework (Al Hadath / Al Arabiya)

[Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/iran/2026/11/09/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B4%D9%86%D8%B7%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-) and Al Hadath, citing **unnamed sources**, reported Monday that a **Qatar-Pakistan** mediation proposal has revived contacts between Washington and Tehran. Per those outlets (also summarized by [Ajel](https://english.ajel.sa/world/r2nhajg55) and [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/world/383960745)), the reported outline includes:

- U.S. acceptance of Iranian **enrichment on Iranian soil up to 3.67%**
- Iran **diluting** its **60%**-enriched uranium stockpile (possibly with help from a third country)
- Release of **$6 billion to $9 billion** in Iranian assets if a deal is reached
- Immediate **reopening of the Strait of Hormuz** and lifting of the maritime blockade
- Arrangements to discuss a return of **IAEA** inspectors (scope not specified)

AFNS has **not** seen a White House, State Department, Qatari, Pakistani, or Iranian government confirmation of those terms.

## Trump to Reuters: peace preferred by Gulf partners; strikes still on the table

President Trump told [Reuters](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/trump-tells-reuters-qatar-saudi-arabia-want-peaceful-iran-deal-us-ready-strikes) on Monday that **"Qatar would rather see a peaceful solution"** and **"Saudi Arabia would rather see a peaceful solution,"** but **"we're ready to go anytime we want"** with strikes. He said he was content for now with the U.S. naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz: **"We'll see where it goes. I'm prepared."**

[Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/09/trump-qatar-prime-minister-iran-war-mediation) reported that Trump met Qatar's prime minister, Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani, for about **45 minutes** on Iran mediation after a separate session with Vice President JD Vance. A diplomat familiar with the Trump and Vance meetings described them as **"positive,"** according to Axios reporting cited in subsequent coverage.
