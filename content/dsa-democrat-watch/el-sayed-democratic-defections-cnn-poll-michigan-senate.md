**Status: Campaign news; poll.** The defections are documented by The Hill and Townhall. Poll figures are from CNN.

## Two Democrats break with El-Sayed

- **Jason Turkish:** the megadonor and fiancé of Rep. Haley Stevens, who lost the primary to El-Sayed, says he will vote for Republican Mike Rogers ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6179793-haley-stevens-fiance-supports-rogers/)). Turkish told The Detroit News that El-Sayed's rhetoric on Gaza "makes folks unsafe," The Hill reports. He was scheduled to attend a Republican Jewish Coalition event with Rogers and Sen. Ted Cruz.
- **Jamie Metzl:** the former Clinton National Security Council official told Democrats to leave the Senate line blank or back Rogers ([Townhall](https://townhall.com/news/amy-curtis/2026/10/28/jamie-metzl-do-not-vote-for-el-sayed-n2685449)).

## El-Sayed's response

Responding to Turkish, El-Sayed said he is "welcoming a big tent" and that people are entitled to their choice ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6180036-el-sayed-responds-stevens-fiance/)).

## The poll

A CNN poll released Thursday shows El-Sayed leading Rogers 49% to 42%, a 7-point lead ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/29/politics/cnn-polls-michigan-maine-texas-kansas-senate-races-midterms-vis)). The election is Nov. 3.

## The House side

Politico reports that the Congressional Progressive Caucus is planning how to use its leverage, while moderates are organizing pushback ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/29/house-democrats-factions-left-center-01140187)).
