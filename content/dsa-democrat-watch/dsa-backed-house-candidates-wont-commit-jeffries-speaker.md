## Five candidates, no commitment

On Election Day, MS NOW reported that five candidates backed by the Democratic Socialists of America are favored, in its assessment, to win safe House seats: Rabb in Pennsylvania's 3rd District, Kiros in Colorado's 1st, McKinney in Michigan's 13th, Avila Chevalier in New York's 13th and Valdez in New York's 7th ([MS NOW](https://www.ms.now/news/dsa-democratic-socialists-house-2026-midterm-elections)).

According to MS NOW, none of the five has committed to backing House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries for speaker. Rabb told the outlet, "I am nobody's rubber stamp," and Avila Chevalier said she is "still thinking about" how she would vote.

## Analysis: possible leverage

If Democrats win a narrow House majority, a group of DSA-aligned members could gain leverage in the speaker vote. That depends on results that are not in yet, on the size of any majority, and on how these candidates ultimately vote.
