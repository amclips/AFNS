**Status: This account is attributed to the New York Times as summarized by Quartz on Nov. 10. AFNS did not independently open the Times article. Ocasio-Cortez has not announced a 2028 campaign. The reporting does not say Mamdani is running for president.**

[Quartz](https://qz.com/mamdani-union-leaders-left-wing-presidential-candidate-2028-111026), summarizing the **New York Times**, reports that New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani has held meetings with union leaders to build early support for a **left-wing 2028 presidential candidate**. Mamdani's aides, the Times reported according to Quartz, said the goal is to identify the best progressive candidate and unite the left behind that person, in order to counter momentum for a more moderate Democrat.

Mamdani met for 45 minutes with **Jimmy Williams Jr.**, head of the International Union of Painters and Allied Trades, at Gracie Mansion, Quartz reported, citing the Times. Nelson, who leads the Association of Flight Attendants-CWA, was another meeting on his schedule. A meeting at Gracie Mansion with **Shawn Fain**, president of the United Auto Workers, is set for **Nov. 11**.

Mamdani spoke of Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez with praise but is preparing an early alternative in case she decides against running, the Times reported, according to Quartz. **Ocasio-Cortez has not announced plans to run in 2028.**

The reporting describes Mamdani as trying to shape the left's 2028 choice. It does not describe him as a presidential candidate.
