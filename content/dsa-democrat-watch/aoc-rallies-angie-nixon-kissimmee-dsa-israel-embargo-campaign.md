**Status: Campaign report.** Poll numbers are attributed to the pollster, and DSA's figures are DSA's own claims.

## The Kissimmee rally

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, Rep. Nydia Velázquez and Rep. Maxwell Frost rallied in Kissimmee for Angie Nixon under the banner "Boricuas for Angie" (WESH).

- **The candidate:** Nixon is a member of the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) and Florida's Democratic nominee for U.S. Senate.
- **The audience:** the event targeted Puerto Rican voters in central Florida.

## The race

- **The poll:** ActiVote has Republican Sen. Ashley Moody ahead 53-47. That is about the same as its mid-October number.
- **The rating:** Sabato's Crystal Ball moved the race from Safe Republican to Likely Republican.
- **The Cuba clip:** Republicans are circulating a clip of Nixon declining to call Cuba a dictatorship (FL Voice News on X).

## DSA's Israel embargo campaign

DSA's "total embargo" campaign on Israel launches Oct. 29 (Hindustan Times).

- **First targets:** United Airlines and Elbit Systems of America.
- **DSA's figures:** DSA has cited cargo-shipment figures, but they haven't been independently verified.

## Why it matters for Americans

A DSA member is the Democratic nominee for a U.S. Senate seat in Florida. DSA is also campaigning against U.S. companies over arms shipments to Israel, a U.S. ally. Both will test how far DSA positions reach into the Democratic Party.
