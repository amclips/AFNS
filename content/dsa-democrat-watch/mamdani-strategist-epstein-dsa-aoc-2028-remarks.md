**Epstein says these are his own views, not DSA's.** After the interview was published, Andrew Epstein told the New York Post that the views he expressed were his own and not those of DSA ([NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/10/28/us-news/mamdani-campaign-guru-admits-dsa-wants-to-take-over-the-democratic-party-by-running-aoc-for-president-in-2028/)).

## What he said

Epstein, a top strategist for Zohran Mamdani, told the Belgian newspaper De Tijd that DSA wants to take over the Democratic Party by running Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez for president in 2028, according to the New York Post's account of the interview.

"You can only change a party by putting forward a president," Epstein said, comparing it to how Trump remade the GOP (NY Post).

## The wider picture

- **Polling:** an Economist/YouGov poll found 50% would vote for a democratic socialist and 41% would not ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6178694-voters-support-democratic-socialist/)).
- **The Nov. 3 ballot:** DSA counts about 200 endorsed candidates and ballot measures ([Democratic Left](https://democraticleft.dsausa.org/2026/10/28/dsa-races-to-watch-on-election-night/)).
- **Races DSA is watching,** per its publication: Nithya Raman for Los Angeles mayor, Gonzales-Torres against Rep. Jimmy Gomez, and Mai Vang against Rep. Doris Matsui.
