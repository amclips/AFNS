**Status: Los Angeles mayor's race uncalled (NBC LA). AOC has not announced 2028 plans.**

## Los Angeles

DSA-backed City Councilmember Nithya Raman has widened her lead over Mayor Karen Bass, [NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/nithya-raman-karen-bass-la-mayor-update-lapd/3966096/) reports. Among about 67% of expected city ballots as of Wednesday afternoon, Raman had 51.7% to Bass's 48.3%, a margin of about 26,000 votes. County officials said roughly 842,000 ballots remained to be processed. NBC LA and the NBC News Decision Desk say the race remains too early to call.

When polls closed Tuesday, Raman was trailing. By Wednesday afternoon she had moved ahead. Jamie McBride, a leader of the LAPD officers' union, said, "If Nithya Raman becomes the mayor, the city needs to prepare for Armageddon," citing her stated plan to fire Chief Jim McDonnell and warning officers would leave the department.

## NYC-DSA and 2028

In New York, [amNY](https://www.amny.com/news/the-peoples-republic-of-astoria-helped-launch-mamdanis-mayoral-campaign-could-it-be-a-launchpad-for-an-aoc-presidential-bid/) reports that NYC-DSA's upcoming citywide convention will vote on whether to urge Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez to run for president in 2028. At the chapter's election-night party in Astoria, co-chair Gustavo Gordillo encouraged members to join that convention vote; the crowd chanted "AOC." amNY also cites an NBC exit poll finding that 18% of voters identify as democratic socialists. Ocasio-Cortez has not announced any 2028 plans.
