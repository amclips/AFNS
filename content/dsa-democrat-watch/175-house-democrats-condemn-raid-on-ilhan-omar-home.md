## The letter

In a letter dated October 26, 175 House Democrats condemned the raid on Rep. Ilhan Omar's home. Reps. Pramila Jayapal, Jamie Raskin, Bennie Thompson, Robert Garcia, Yvette Clarke and Greg Casar led the effort.

## The demand

The letter demands that DOJ and DHS take no further investigative steps that could affect the upcoming November 3 election.

**No charges have been filed against Omar.**
