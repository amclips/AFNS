**Status: Polls; not results.** These are New York Times/Siena College polls of likely voters, taken six days before the Nov. 3 election. Polls are snapshots, not predictions.

## South Carolina: tied

Democrat Annie Andrews and appointed Republican Sen. Darline Graham are at 48% each (Siena; Political Wire).

## Minnesota: Flanagan ahead

Progressive Democrat Peggy Flanagan leads former broadcaster Michele Tafoya, a Republican, 54-40. The Times calls the result one of its biggest outliers.

## Florida: Moody ahead

Republican Sen. Ashley Moody leads Democratic state Rep. Angie Nixon 50-44. ActiVote had Moody up 53-47. For Nixon's campaign and DSA ties, see [AOC rallies for Angie Nixon in Florida](../aoc-rallies-angie-nixon-kissimmee-dsa-israel-embargo-campaign/).
