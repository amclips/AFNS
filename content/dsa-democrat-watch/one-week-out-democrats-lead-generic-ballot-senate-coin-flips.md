## The national picture

In the Reuters/Ipsos poll, Democrats lead 53% to 44% on the generic ballot among likely voters. Trump's approval is at 32%, and only 20% of voters approve of him on the cost of living.

## Why analysts urge caution

A Reuters analysis warns that only 40 to 50 House seats are truly competitive and that Republicans hold a large cash edge, so a Senate majority is far from certain. Democrats need four more Senate seats and three more House seats. A 50-50 Senate stays Republican because of Vance's tiebreaking vote.

## Senate races to watch

- **Texas:** Democrat James Talarico and Republican Ken Paxton are tied in Fox News and Rasmussen polling, with more than 4.5 million early votes already cast. Talarico raised $17.6M in the first two weeks of October to Paxton's $2.8M.
- **Georgia:** A Quantus poll has Sen. Jon Ossoff ahead of Mike Collins 51.8 to 44.9.
- **New Hampshire:** Rasmussen has Chris Pappas leading John Sununu 50 to 44 ahead of Wednesday's WMUR debate.
- **Michigan:** A Michigan State/YouGov poll has Abdul El-Sayed leading Mike Rogers 48 to 46.

## Money and infighting

Democratic candidates are outraising Republicans, but the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee (DSCC) took out a $15M loan and spent $32M in early October across 10 states. Meanwhile, progressives and establishment Democrats are clashing over whether to fund left-flank candidates like El-Sayed and Florida's Angie Nixon.
