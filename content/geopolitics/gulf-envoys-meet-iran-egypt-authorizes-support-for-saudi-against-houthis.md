## Envoys in Tehran

Saudi, Pakistani and Turkish envoys met Iran's leaders on Monday to try to stop the war from restarting. Iran called the talks "very good," but the only thing agreed was to keep talking. Brent crude is around $116, according to the AP and PBS reports.

## Egypt and the Houthis

Egypt's parliament authorized defensive military support for Saudi Arabia against the Houthis if Riyadh asks for it. No request has been made yet.

A Houthi leader called the move a "declaration of war." Saudi Arabia says it shot down three ballistic missiles and four drones.
