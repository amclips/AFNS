## 1. Iran and the Strait of Hormuz

Three tankers were reported hit in or near the Strait of Hormuz. A **UK maritime security** agency reported an explosion off Oman, and the **IRGC** said tankers struck mines on "unauthorised" routes ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/6/iran-war-live-trump-warns-us-may-have-to-destroy-rest-of-iran), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-news-november-6-2026-ab62071e056c046fe6034216e14b8bb7), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/06/live-iran-war-irgc-strait-of-hormuz/)). About a third of the world's seaborne oil passes through Hormuz; G7 countries agreed to release oil stocks, those outlets report. Trump's sharper Oval Office warnings are covered on our Iran story. **UNCONFIRMED:** [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/06/live-iran-war-irgc-strait-of-hormuz/) says Tehran reportedly sent a nuclear proposal to Washington via Qatar — treat that line as unconfirmed here. Iran's Mohsen Rezaei met Vladimir Putin in Moscow ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/06/live-iran-war-irgc-strait-of-hormuz/)).

## 2. Israel's next government

President Herzog will announce who gets the first mandate on **Sunday**. Gadi Eisenkot has recommendations from **67 of 120** seats versus **46** for Benjamin Netanyahu ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)). **Sixty-seven recommendations are not a governing coalition** — Arab-party recommenders have not committed to join, and stalemate or another election remains possible. Full nuance: [Herzog to Name Mandate Sunday; Eisenkot Has 67 Recommendations but Not Yet a Coalition](/israel/herzog-certifies-election-results-coalition-consultations-begin/).

## 3. Gaza and the West Bank

Israeli strikes killed at least five Palestinians in central Gaza, including a 12-year-old boy, overnight coverage says ([Ouest-France](https://www.ouest-france.fr/monde/iran/guerre-au-moyen-orient-gadi-eisenkot-favori-en-israel-bombardements-meurtriers-a-gaza-le-point-sur-la-nuit-0387222a-d993-11f1-a45f-49114163a350), [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)). Both sides accuse each other of breaking the October 2025 ceasefire. The **Gaza Health Ministry** says more than **1,600** people have been killed since the truce began — that figure is **attributed to the ministry**, not independently verified here. The UN condemned a settler takeover of Palestinian homes near Jericho ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)).

## 4. Yemen and Saudi Arabia

Houthis tried to hit Saudi Arabia's Madinah region with a ballistic missile; Gulf states and Jordan condemned the attack ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)). WHO puts deaths in Yemen since August at 1,771. Türkiye's parliament ratified a defense pact with Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, redrawing regional security alignments ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)).

## 5. Russia–Ukraine diplomacy

Trump met privately with Putin envoy **Kirill Dmitriev** and predicted a "very big" deal that could involve Europe ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/russia/trump-says-met-putin-envoy-sees-ukraine-deal-soon-rcna607804)). U.S.-mediated Russia–Ukraine talks are set for **Nov. 10-11 in Abu Dhabi**. Allies spent two days in Brussels on postwar security guarantees; the EU sent Ukraine €4.4 billion ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/ukraine/russia-ukraine-exchange-airstrikes-us-envoy-describes-renewed-security-rcna607778)).

## 6. Russia–Ukraine fighting

Zelenskyy said every Ukrainian power plant has been damaged by Russian attacks ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/06/ukraine-war-briefing-zelenskyy-says-russia-has-damaged-every-power-plant-as-he-seeks-latin-american-help)). The death toll from Monday's bombing of an aid point in Zaporizhzhia rose to **53**. Ukraine carried out drone strikes on southern Russia, including the Afipsky refinery area; Russia said it shot down nearly 500 drones ([Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/ukraine-russia-war-live-trump-putin-zelensky-uk-g20-melania-b3080221.html)).

## 7. Leadership turmoil elsewhere

**Paraguay's President Santiago Peña resigned** over the collapse of digital lender Ueno Bank ([Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261106/12f8326e8a914ff78c0cd55262096086/c.html), [Nhan Dan](https://en.nhandan.vn/world-news-in-brief-november-6-post168765.html), [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-6-2026/4113576)). In France, about 600,000 protested the cost of living, with 400-plus arrests. At least 18 flights faced bomb threats in Europe; Sweden plans to bring back internal border checks on Nov. 12.
