## Iran: Qeshm strike and Qatar's mediation

President Trump called the U.S. strike on Qeshm Island a minor strike on an anti-aircraft site. The WSJ says it was one of several limited, secret U.S. strikes in recent weeks. This is the strike AFNS reported Nov. 2-3; the sources do not describe a new one. Iran says it can hit back, and that the Strait of Hormuz will reopen only if Washington meets its demands, including lifting the U.S. naval blockade. Qatar is carrying proposals between the sides, and gaps remain ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/middle-east-war-10-things-uae-residents-need-to-know-tonight-november-3-1.500734614), [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/163177/early-edition-november-3-2026/), [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261104/9d60055ecbb94951b72f77f8ca147b9f/c.html)). Our full story: [U.S. Officials: Limited 'Defensive' Strikes Hit Iranian Missiles on Qeshm Island](/israel/report-us-strikes-iranian-missile-launcher-qeshm-island/).

## Trump says U.S. military aid to Israel will end

Trump says U.S. military aid to Israel will end. Gulf News and Anadolu put the aid at about $3.8 billion a year under a 10-year agreement that runs to 2028. Haaretz, in our earlier story, cited roughly $4 billion a year. Israeli officials were caught off guard, no details have been released, and the money is still flowing. Separately, Shas and two far-right parties are backing Netanyahu ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/middle-east-war-10-things-uae-residents-need-to-know-tonight-november-3-1.500734614), [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-4-2026/4110977)). Our full story: [Report: Trump Says U.S. Military Aid to Israel Will End This Year](/israel/report-trump-says-us-military-aid-israel-will-end-this-year/).

## Ukraine: Russia's biggest attack on the grid this year

Overnight into Nov. 3, Russia launched 289 drones, 17 ballistic missiles and 59 cruise missiles at Ukraine's energy system, according to the [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-launches-biggest-mass-attack-on-ukraines-energy-infrastructure-this-year/), its biggest attack on the grid this year. About 10 regions lost power. Drones targeted a substation serving the Khmelnytskyi nuclear plant. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/at-least-73-injured-as-russia-bombs-ukrainian-hospital-and-power-plants) reports a strike on a hospital in Kharkiv injured 73. The Nov. 2 strike on an aid site at a school in Zaporizhzhia region has killed more than 30 people, with dozens missing, according to the reports. Ukraine struck the Yaroslavl refinery in Russia ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/03/g-s1-151439/russia-ukraine-war)).

## Abu Dhabi talks set for Nov. 10-11

The Kremlin confirmed the talks in Abu Dhabi, with Vladimir Medinsky leading its delegation. U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff says the agenda covers postwar security, de-escalation and humanitarian issues. The latest strikes followed the longest local truce of the war, in Kherson ([RTE](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1103/1597730-ukraine/), [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/us-russia-ukraine-peace-talks-abu-dhabi-ceasefire/33889451.html)).

## Yemen: Houthis near Aden

The Houthis are within about 50 miles of Aden, the closest they have come since 2015, Reuters reports citing military sources. Saudi Arabia says it shot down drones, including over the Mecca region, and the U.S. Embassy in Riyadh issued a warning about attacks on airports (as relayed by [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/163177/early-edition-november-3-2026/), [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261104/9d60055ecbb94951b72f77f8ca147b9f/c.html) and [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-4-2026/4110977)).

## Gaza: the ceasefire frays

[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/israeli-forces-kill-four-in-gaza-as-fifth-palestinian-dies-of-wounds) reports Israeli strikes killed at least four people on Monday. Gaza's health ministry says about 1,600 people have been killed since the ceasefire of October 2025. On Tuesday, Israel turned back travelers and stopped aid trucks at Rafah, and Nickolay Mladenov, the Board of Peace's high representative, was in Israel pushing for winter tents ([Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261104/9d60055ecbb94951b72f77f8ca147b9f/c.html)). Our earlier story: [Report: Israel Fully Closes Rafah Crossing Into Gaza, Halting Aid and Goods](/israel/report-israel-fully-closes-rafah-crossing-gaza-aid-goods/).

## Europe: cyberattacks

Hackers took remote control of supermarket fridges and freezers in several countries, and Italy held emergency talks. A French blood-bank system and the bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa were also attacked. No one has been named as responsible ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/04)).

- Whether Russia keeps hitting Ukraine's grid before the Abu Dhabi talks on Nov. 10
