## Israel: the count continues

No camp has a majority yet, and counting is ongoing. For the latest official figures, see [the updated Israel election article](../../israel/israel-election-no-clear-winner-netanyahu-eisenkot-claim-victory/).

## Iran: report of a possible campaign after the midterms

The Wall Street Journal reports that Washington told several Gulf states to expect a "short, intense military campaign" against Iran after Nov. 3, with Patriot air defenses moving in (WSJ; Anadolu).

- **More forces:** the WSJ says another carrier group is due next month.
- **No decision yet:** President Trump has not decided. At a rally he said, "You'll see Iran in a couple of weeks."
- **The White House** declined to comment.

For Israel's reaction and the regional talks in Tehran, see [the Israel desk's Iran report](../../israel/us-israel-signal-iran-campaign-after-midterms-tehran-talks/).

## Hormuz: tanker hits mines

- **The tanker:** Iran's army says an oil tanker caught fire after hitting mines on a route Iran hasn't authorized (Gulf News).
- **Iran's conditions:** Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei says Iran's conditions, including control of the strait, must be met before the standoff ends (Gulf News).
- **UNCONFIRMED:** Posts trending on X say Qatar has given Tehran a seven-day plan to reopen the strait, but major outlets haven't reported it.

## Houthis and Saudi Arabia: Egypt steps in

- **Egypt:** Egypt's parliament authorized President el-Sisi to send defensive military support to Saudi Arabia. It rules out strikes on Yemen and ground troops (AP; Ahram Online).
- **The Houthi response:** the Houthis called it "a declaration of war."
- **Ras Tanura:** the Houthis claim a missile hit Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery. Saudi Arabia hasn't confirmed this, though Bloomberg reported that a fire near the terminal was contained (as cited by Middle East Online).

## Ukraine: the Oleshky truce holds

- **No violations:** both sides report no violations (AP; The Guardian; ISW).
- **Purpose:** the truce is meant to let about 2,000 trapped civilians leave Oleshky.
- **End:** it runs until 11 p.m. Kyiv time on Nov. 1 (3 p.m. CT).
- **Kyiv strike:** hours before the truce started, Russian missiles hit Kyiv, killing at least one person.

## Putin's call with Nordic leaders

- **Putin's position:** Putin told Finland's president and Norway's prime minister that NATO expansion and other "root causes" must be dealt with before any peace deal (ISW; The Guardian).
- **Kyiv's view:** Kyiv hopes for "new momentum."
- **UNCONFIRMED:** Posts on X say Moscow will demand NATO pull forces back to 1997 levels.

## Brazil: Flávio Bolsonaro reportedly in Miami

Flávio Bolsonaro reportedly flew to Miami despite a court travel ban, after President Lula beat him in Sunday's runoff. A senior State Department official had said the U.S. would grant visas to him and his family, which could spark a Brazil-U.S. dispute (Anadolu).

- **Hormuz:** whether major outlets confirm the reported Qatar plan (UNCONFIRMED, posts trending on X).
- **Ukraine:** whether the Oleshky evacuation finishes before the truce ends.
