## Ukraine and Russia — prisoner swap, and overnight strikes

U.S.-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, the first in this format since February, ended with both sides confirming agreement on a large prisoner exchange. Jared Kushner called it "the biggest humanitarian release of prisoners," with numbers to be finalized Thursday, and said he and Steve Witkoff would meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow on Thursday, then see Volodymyr Zelenskyy separately. Territory and security guarantees were not resolved. Overnight, Russia said it downed hundreds of Ukrainian drones and reported a fire at the Ust-Luga port. Full story: [U.S.-Mediated Talks in Abu Dhabi End With Agreement on a Large Prisoner Swap; Fighting Continued Overnight](/geopolitics/us-russia-ukraine-talks-abu-dhabi-agree-large-prisoner-swap/).

## G7 in Paris

G7 foreign ministers, after meetings on Nov. 9-10, backed U.S.-led efforts to facilitate peace through direct talks, called for a full, reciprocal ceasefire beginning with a halt to strikes on energy infrastructure and in the Black Sea, and said negotiations on Europe's security must include Europeans. They condemned Iran's obstruction of the Strait of Hormuz and opposed tolls or other limits on passage. Canada reimposed sanctions on 11 Iranian entities. Full story: [G7 Foreign Ministers in Paris Back U.S.-Led Ukraine Talks and Say Europe Must Have a Seat](/geopolitics/g7-foreign-ministers-paris-back-us-ukraine-talks-condemn-hormuz-disruption/).

## France — supermarket cyberattack attribution

France on Monday attributed an October cyberattack on refrigeration controls at more than 700 supermarket sites to Shahid Kaveh, a unit it says is affiliated with Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. Iran's foreign ministry called the accusation "baseless and hostile." Full story: [France Attributes a Cyberattack on Supermarket Refrigeration Systems to Iran's Revolutionary Guard](/geopolitics/france-attributes-supermarket-refrigeration-cyberattack-to-iran-irgc/).

## Taiwan and APEC

Taiwan named Acer founder Stan Shih as its envoy to next week's APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen and asked China for a written guarantee of equal, safe participation. Beijing has tied attendance to the "one-China principle." Full story: [Taiwan Names Acer Founder Stan Shih as APEC Envoy and Asks China for a Written Guarantee](/geopolitics/taiwan-names-stan-shih-apec-envoy-asks-china-written-guarantee/).

## Iran, Israel, and Gaza — see desk stories

Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei said the Strait of Hormuz will stay shut until the United States meets Tehran's conditions, and he denied that talks with Washington are underway, even as President Trump has described "positive momentum." UKMTO has reported incidents involving two tankers. Those developments are in [Iran Reports Tanker Blasts in the Strait of Hormuz](/israel/iran-reports-tanker-blasts-strait-of-hormuz-ukmto-confirms-incident/) and [Trump Says Iran War Is Ending 'Fairly Soon'; Bessent Moved to a Military Base After Iranian Threats, Reports Say](/trump-administration/trump-says-iran-war-ending-fairly-soon-bessent-moved-after-iranian-threats/).
