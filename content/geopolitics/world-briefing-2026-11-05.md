## Hormuz: Iran says tankers hit mines

Iran says oil tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after ships tried an "unauthorized route." The IRGC says two oil tankers and a tugboat caught fire Wednesday night and that one tanker struck a mine ([Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/morning-briefing-nov-5-2026/4112214), [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/morning-update-660)). **UNCONFIRMED:** other reports quote the Guard as saying four "rogue" tankers were destroyed; the number is not confirmed. Brent crude was trading around $95.

## U.S.-Iran talks after the midterms

Qatar says both sides are reviewing proposals and that "bridging the gap is still a work in progress" ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-war-yemen-houthis-lebanon-israel-ada824e7387edec2cd8d1f97045dff2b), [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261104/9d60055ecbb94951b72f77f8ca147b9f/c.html)). Iranian media say Tehran may skip Monday's Pakistan talks that Trump ordered, days before a ceasefire expires. Iranian officials read Democrats' midterm win as a vote against the war. Trump told the Financial Times the U.S. is "locked and loaded" but may not need to strike (as relayed in regional coverage).

## Ukraine: Kyiv without water; school strike toll rises

Nov. 3 Russian strikes caused Kyiv's worst water outage of the full-scale war, with much of the city without water for more than 24 hours ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-kyiv-suffers-worst-water-outage-of-full-scale-war-after-mass-russian-assault/)). The death toll from a Nov. 2 strike on an aid point at a school in Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhzhia, rose to 42; reports say Russia used a three-ton bomb ([Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/international/4002095-reuters-world-news-summary) / Reuters summary).

## Abu Dhabi talks Nov. 10-11

Allies are preparing for technical-level Ukraine-Russia-U.S. talks in Abu Dhabi on Nov. 10-11 that could lead to a leaders' meeting ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-partners-coordinate-peace-efforts-ahead-of-abu-dhabi-talks-with-russia-2/), Anadolu). Zelensky held a 90-minute call with Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner on air defense and postwar security guarantees; the EU is planning talks in Brussels. Moscow still demands the Donbas. China says it is discussing a possible summit this month of Chinese, Russian and U.S. leaders.

## Israel: Eisenkot backing and Rafah

Israel's opposition is backing Gadi Eisenkot for the mandate as President Herzog holds coalition consultations, and Israel has closed the Rafah crossing to travelers, aid and goods ([Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261104/9d60055ecbb94951b72f77f8ca147b9f/c.html), [Xinhua Rafah](https://english.news.cn/20261104/a417131bbc594c638346f60ea03e6c86/c.html), [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2041990)). ## Houthis and Saudi Arabia

**UNCONFIRMED:** the Houthis claim hits on a Riyadh airport and air bases in southwestern Saudi Arabia. Saudi forces say they shot down drones, including one over the Mecca region. Airstrikes were reported on Houthi depots in Al-Bayda ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-iran-war-yemen-houthis-lebanon-israel-ada824e7387edec2cd8d1f97045dff2b), [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261104/9d60055ecbb94951b72f77f8ca147b9f/c.html)).

## Europe: second wave of bomb threats

A second wave of bomb threats hit flights across Europe, with at least 15 flights diverted or making emergency landings; none of the threats were real ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/05/all)). The wave revived fears of "hybrid" sabotage that European officials have blamed on Russia and Iran; both deny involvement.
