## Ukraine: drone strike on Dnipro's railway station

A Russian jet-powered drone hit a crowded platform at Dnipro's railway station, where a train to Zaporizhzhia was waiting. DW reports at least 13 dead, and the Kyiv Post reports 14. An 11-year-old boy is among the critically injured. President Zelenskyy called the strike "horrific" ([DW](https://www.dw.com/en/ukraine-13-killed-in-russian-drone-strike-on-crowded-dnipro-train-station/a-79795076); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/88526)).

## Ukraine: the Oleshky truce holds for now

- **No violations:** more than 30 hours have passed without a reported violation (ISW; The Guardian).
- **The evacuation:** residents who leave are supposed to travel through Anapa in Russia to Gomel in Belarus on Oct. 31. It is unclear how they would get back to Ukrainian-held territory.
- **ISW's warning:** ISW warns Russia may use the pause to regroup.

## North Korea fires about 10 missiles

North Korea fired about 10 short-range ballistic missiles into the sea off its east coast, South Korea says. It was the North's first launch in 17 days ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/asia/north-korea-fires-10-ballistic-missiles-sea-east-coast-seoul-says-rcna606273); AP).

## Gaza and Yemen

- **Gaza:** local health officials say Israeli strikes in southern Gaza killed at least four people on Wednesday, including near a Red Cross field hospital in Rafah, despite the ceasefire (AP). For more, see the [Israel morning briefing](../../israel/israel-morning-briefing-2026-10-29/).
- **Yemen (UNCONFIRMED):** the Houthis claim airstrikes killed 14 people at a market in northern Yemen (AP). The claim has not been independently confirmed.

## Black Sea: cargo ship hit off Bulgaria

Three unmanned boats hit the container ship Contship Jet off Bulgaria, a NATO member, on Oct. 28. The ship later sank, and all 18 crew were rescued (AP). **UNCONFIRMED ATTRIBUTION:** ISW says the boats were possibly Russian. Nobody has confirmed who launched them.

## Iran
