**Status: Voting; results pending.** Exit polls come out this afternoon CT, and official results are due November 4.

## What's at stake

Today's vote is Israel's first election since October 7. Polls show Gadi Eisenkot's party competing with or ahead of Likud, and neither bloc on track for the 61 seats needed to govern. Netanyahu could stay in office for months while parties negotiate a coalition.

## The final polls

- **Maariv** (released October 23): opposition bloc 54 seats, Netanyahu's bloc 48, Arab parties 14. Neither side would reach 61.
- **Times of Israel:** Eisenkot's Yashar party first with 23 seats, Likud second with 21. The poll came a few days after Trump endorsed Netanyahu.
- **Preferred prime minister:** Eisenkot leads Netanyahu 49% to 41%.

The exit polls, the official results on November 4, and coalition talks.
