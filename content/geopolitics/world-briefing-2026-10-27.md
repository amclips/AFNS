## Middle East

- **Lead:** [Iran turns up the pressure on Hormuz as Washington holds off until the midterms](../iran-hormuz-pressure-us-holds-off-until-midterms/). Ship attacks hit a record in October. The IRGC's claim that seven tankers were damaged in one night is **unverified**, and US Central Command disputes the mine claims.
- [Israel votes today](../israel-votes-first-election-since-october-7/) (**voting; results pending**). It's the first election since October 7. Neither bloc is polling at the 61 seats needed to govern. Exit polls come out this afternoon CT.
- [Gulf envoys meet Iran; Egypt authorizes support for Saudi Arabia](../gulf-envoys-meet-iran-egypt-authorizes-support-for-saudi-against-houthis/). Saudi, Pakistani and Turkish envoys met Iran's leaders Monday; the only thing agreed was to keep talking. Egypt's parliament authorized defensive military support for Saudi Arabia against the Houthis if Riyadh asks. No request has been made yet.

## Russia–Ukraine

- [Russia's offensive stalls as the Oleshky truce begins and talks head to Abu Dhabi](../russia-offensive-stalls-oleshky-truce-abu-dhabi-talks/). ISW assesses a net Russian loss of ground since September, while Putin claims big gains. The Oleshky truce is holding. Ukraine's reported strike on a glide-bomb battery plant is **unconfirmed**.

## Elsewhere

- [Serbia](../serbia-student-movement-wins-majority-preliminary-results/) (**preliminary results**): the student-led movement won 52.7% and a majority in parliament; Vučić conceded.
- [Brazil](../brazil-lula-wins-fourth-term-bolsonaro-rejects-result/): Lula won a fourth term, 51.8% to 48.2%. Flávio Bolsonaro rejects the result and wants it annulled.

## What to watch

Israel's exit polls, whether Saudi Arabia formally asks Egypt for help, the EU–Canada summit on October 29–30, and the US midterms on November 3.

*Note on oil prices: the AP/PBS reports on Monday's talks put Brent crude around $116; the CBS reporting in our Hormuz article puts it at about $112–113, its highest since May. The figures come from different reports and times.*
