**Status: Posts documented; clip unverified.** Fuentes's own X posts are linked below. The clip described by X's automated news summary is **UNVERIFIED**. AFNS has not reviewed it.

**A note on the name:** "America First," as used by Nick Fuentes, refers to his movement and its branding. AFNS uses "America First" in its own tagline but has no affiliation with Fuentes or his movement.

The Washington Times calls Fuentes an extremist, and Newsweek calls him a far-right Gen Z influencer.

## "Nobody to blame but themselves"

On Oct. 27, Fuentes wrote on X that Trump had won "an historic mandate" and a trifecta. He went on: "they blew it all away by fighting Israel's war. THEY HAVE NOBODY TO BLAME BUT THEMSELVES." The post drew about 1 million views and 48,000 likes.

## Reply to Rep. Gill

Rep. Brandon Gill (R-Texas) posted, "I am so sick of Democrats trying to sell my country to foreigners." Fuentes replied, "I'm sick of Republicans marrying them." His reply drew about 14,000 likes.

## UNVERIFIED: the "start from zero" clip

**UNVERIFIED:** X's automated news summary says a clip shows Fuentes welcoming a leftist electoral wave. According to the summary, he said leaders should be jailed so the right could "start from zero." The leaders it names are Trump, Netanyahu, Bolsonaro, Milei, Meloni, Orbán and Farage. AFNS has not reviewed the clip.

Conservatives on X accused him of sabotage.

## Critics push back

According to X's summary, critics called Fuentes a poor predictor and a "useful idiot" for the left. Commentator Ashton Forbes called him the "Jim Cramer of politics."

## On his show

On his Rumble show after midnight, Fuentes said he would cover Israel's election, with "Netanyahu clinging to power until APRIL." He also said he would cover a meeting between "the Mecca Pact and Iran."
