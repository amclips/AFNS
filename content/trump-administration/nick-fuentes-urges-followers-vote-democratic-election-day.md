**Status: Reported by the Forward and Vanity Fair; impact not measured.**

On the morning of the midterms, streamer Nick Fuentes told his followers: "Get out and vote Democrat today." He framed it as a tactical protest over the Iran war, Israel policy, the Epstein files and deportations, not a shift to the left, according to [the Forward](https://forward.com/news/860210/nick-fuentes-2026-midterms-democrats-endorsement-antisemitism-groyper/).

## From "veto" to backing Democrats

Fuentes, who broke with President Trump over the Iran war, went from urging his audience to withhold their votes to openly backing Democrats. "I've been very open about why I'm not supporting Trump, and that's because he started a war with Iran," he told [Vanity Fair](https://www.vanityfair.com/story/nick-fuentes-midterms-trump) on Oct. 29, describing his followers' votes as a "veto." "We can exercise the veto, and I think that's the source of our power," he said.

By Tuesday morning, his message was "Get out and vote Democrat today." On his Rumble show, he pushed votes for Democrats in House and Senate races and called the move tactical, not ideological. He said he would accept the blame if Republicans suffered heavy losses (Forward).

Democrats are now projected to win both chambers of Congress. How much his audience moved votes hasn't been measured.

In Ohio, Fuentes had urged his followers to back Democrat Amy Acton over Vivek Ramaswamy for governor, in an Oct. 8 post that drew more than 2.1 million views ([Cincinnati Exchange](https://thecincinnatiexchange.com/groyper-movement-ohio-acton-ramaswamy/)). AP lists Acton as the winner ([ABC News live updates](https://abcnews.com/Politics/live-updates/midterm-elections-live-updates/?entryId=137840691&id=137782085)). No one has measured whether his audience affected the result.
