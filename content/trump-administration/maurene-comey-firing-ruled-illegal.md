**Status: Ruling; DOJ to appeal** (Maurene Comey). **Charged (alleged)** (James Comey): he has not been convicted, and his lawyers have moved to dismiss.

## Maurene Comey: firing ruled unlawful

Judge Jesse Furman of the Southern District of New York ruled Monday that DOJ unlawfully fired Maurene Comey, James Comey's daughter, as a federal prosecutor. He ordered her reinstated retroactively with back pay and rejected the department's argument that Article II of the Constitution let it fire her. DOJ says it will appeal.

## James Comey: a third charge

The same day, at James Comey's request, a sealed charging document filed October 5 was unsealed. It charges him with passing national defense information to a reporter in 2016, plus conspiracy. It is the third time in a little over a year that DOJ has tried to charge him.

His lawyers argue the case is time-barred because the 10-year statute of limitations has run out, and they have moved to dismiss. More on the charge: [James Comey charged a third time](../james-comey-charged-third-time/).
