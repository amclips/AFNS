**Status: Reported by Reuters.** Reuters' report ran on Nov. 1, before the vote, and cites a White House official and two people familiar with the plans. We link the Reuters text as syndicated by [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/politics-news/trump-white-house-maps-path-to-govern-without-congress-if-democrats-win-4989653) and as republished by [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/global-news/trump-white-house-prepares-to-govern-without-congress-if-democrats-win-midterm-elections-2026-11-03-140341).

Senior White House officials have spent months identifying actions President Trump could take without Congress, on tariffs, visas, housing and spending, in case Democrats took control and launched investigations, Reuters reports. Democrats are now projected to win both chambers ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/midterm-elections-2026-live-updates.html)).

## Who is planning, and what's on the list

According to Reuters, deputy chief of staff Stephen Miller and Office of Management and Budget Director Russell Vought have worked with the White House Counsel's Office since April. The options include new tariffs on imports of critical minerals and wind turbines and tighter visa rules for international students and temporary foreign workers.

Vought is reviewing which priorities can move forward with money Congress has already appropriated. Reuters says he also wants to change federal grant rules to give political appointees more say over which programs get funding.

## "Mr. Veto"

Trump said he expects to issue vetoes for "a month or two" before reaching compromises with Democrats, Reuters reports.
