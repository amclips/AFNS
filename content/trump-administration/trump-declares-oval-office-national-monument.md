**Status: White House proclamation.** Covered by [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmy0eeg101djo) and [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6199942-live-updates-trump-arch-midterm-elections-russia-ukraine/). This is a **proclamation**, not an executive order.

President Trump signed a [proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/11/establishment-of-the-oval-office-national-monument/) under the **Antiquities Act** establishing the Oval Office, Outer Oval Office and Cabinet Room as a national monument managed by the National Park Service through the Interior Department. The White House text says the monument covers **Rooms 110, 106, 108 and 105**.

Substantial alteration or demolition would require documentation and a review of alternatives. The proclamation does not bar presidents from **redecorating** or making routine **security** changes, and it does not limit Congress's authority over federal property.

Trump said he would send the proclamation to Congress for ratification, [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6199942-live-updates-trump-arch-midterm-elections-russia-ukraine/) reports. Per the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmy0eeg101djo), he said the rooms will "not be allowed to be touched by anybody, destroyed, ripped down by stupid people, in order to build something in a slightly different shape."
