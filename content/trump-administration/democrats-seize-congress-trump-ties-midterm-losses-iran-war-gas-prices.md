**Status: Confirmed by Reuters, NBC News and the Washington Post.** Seat counts are stated as "at least," with Mississippi and Alaska still undecided.

Democrats won majorities in both the House and the Senate in the Nov. 3 midterms, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/legal/government/democrats-seize-congress-sweeping-midterm-repudiation-trump-2026-11-04/). Reuters reports Democrats hold at least 54 Senate seats and gained at least 30 House seats, with Mississippi and Alaska still undecided. Voters were angry over high prices and the U.S.-Iran war, Reuters says, and Democrats vowed to use their new majorities to rein in the president, setting up possible gridlock.

The [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/04/election-2026-live-updates-democrats-take-control-house-senate-trump-distances-himself-historic-losses/) described the outcome as the Republican Party's worst losses in half a century and reported that House Speaker Mike Johnson will not seek to remain GOP leader, according to a top aide.

## Trump on Vance and the war

President Trump made his first post-election appearance at the White House ballroom construction site. Asked about Vice President JD Vance's assessment that the Iran war cost Republicans the midterms, Trump told [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/trump-election-white-house-ballroom-iran-rcna607487): "I think it's largely true because it drove the gasoline up a little bit short term," but added, "if I had it to do again, I'd have to do it again because long term it's the only answer. You can't let Iran have a nuclear weapon." On the race to replace Johnson, he said, "We'll stay out of it."
