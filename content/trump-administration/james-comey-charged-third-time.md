**Status: Charged (alleged).** Comey has not been convicted. His lawyers have moved to dismiss.

## What happened

At James Comey's request, a sealed charging document filed October 5 was unsealed Monday. It charges him with passing national defense information to a reporter in 2016, plus conspiracy. It is the third time in a little over a year that the Justice Department has tried to charge him.

## His defense

Comey's lawyers argue the case is time-barred because the 10-year statute of limitations has run out, and they have moved to dismiss.
