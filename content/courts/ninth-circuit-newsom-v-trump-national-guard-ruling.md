## Background

In 2025, President Trump federalized the California National Guard in response to protests in Los Angeles, over Gov. Gavin Newsom's objection. California sued, and the order blocking the federalization was challenged on appeal.

## The ruling

On October 23, 2026, a divided 9th Circuit panel upheld that order. Writing for the majority, Judge Jennifer Sung concluded the president exceeded his statutory authority and that the protests did not rise to the level of a "rebellion." Judge Miller concurred, and Judge Bennett dissented.

## Why it matters

The Guard troops are no longer deployed, so the ruling's main weight is as precedent for future fights over a president using the Guard against a governor's wishes.
