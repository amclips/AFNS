**Status: 14-day TRO granted Nov. 5; reported Nov. 6.** Source: [Courthouse News](https://courthousenews.com/feds-barred-from-pressing-washington-state-to-toss-voters-ballots-for-now/).

U.S. District Judge **Jamal Whitehead** (W.D. Wash.) partly granted a temporary restraining order in **OneAmerica Votes v. DOJ**, barring the Justice Department, Homeland Security, Attorney General **Todd Blanche** and DHS Secretary **Markwayne Mullin** from demanding that Washington set aside about **400** ballots of suspected noncitizens or threatening election officials who count them.

The court drew a key line: the government **"may ask a state to set ballots aside, but it may not compel one to do so."** President Trump is **not** covered by the order. Whitehead refused a stay pending appeal. A hearing is set for **Nov. 18**; counties certify by **Nov. 24**. Courthouse News notes the order creates a Washington-specific restriction after the Ninth Circuit's en banc court paused the nationwide order in SEIU v. DOJ.
