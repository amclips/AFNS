## What happened

On October 21, the Supreme Court refused the Trump administration's request to pause an order by a federal judge in Washington, D.C. That order blocks a Bureau of Prisons policy that cuts off hormone therapy and other accommodations for transgender inmates.

## Why it matters

With the request denied, the lower court's order blocking the policy stays in place.

## The vote

Justices Alito and Thomas would have granted the administration's request.
