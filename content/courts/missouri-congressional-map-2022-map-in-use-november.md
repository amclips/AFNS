*Background: these rulings came in September.*

## What happened

The Missouri Supreme Court unanimously ruled that the 2025 congressional map drawn by Republicans never took effect, because of a referendum petition.

The U.S. Supreme Court then blocked a federal judge's order that would have required use of the new map.

## What's next

The 2022 map is in use for the November election.
