## What happened

On October 22, the Supreme Court paused a federal judge's ruling, which means New Hampshire can bar the use of student IDs as voter identification in this election.

## Why it matters

The Court cited the rule against changing election rules close to an election.

## The vote

Justice Jackson was the lone dissenter.
