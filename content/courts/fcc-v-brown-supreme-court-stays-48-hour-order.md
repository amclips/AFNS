## What happened

On October 14, the U.S. Supreme Court, in an unsigned opinion in *FCC v. Brown*, put on hold an order from the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit. That order would have forced the Federal Communications Commission to rule on Sherrod Brown's complaint over a campaign ad within 48 hours.

## Why it matters

The Court said judges can't rush an agency's deliberations like that.

## The vote

Justices Sotomayor and Jackson would have denied the stay.
