## What happened

On October 19, the Supreme Court declined to take up *O'Donnell v. Chicago*, a challenge to the city of Chicago's practice of seizing cars over as few as two unpaid parking tickets, without compensating the owners.

## Separate statement

Justice Sotomayor, joined by Justice Gorsuch, flagged "serious concerns" about the practice.
